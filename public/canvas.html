How to paste into Slack Canvas
1
Click Generate from Live Data above.
2
Click Copy Plain Text for Slack.
3
Open your Slack canvas → click inside → Ctrl+A → Delete to clear old content.
4
Press Ctrl+V to paste. Slack renders headings and bullet lists automatically.
