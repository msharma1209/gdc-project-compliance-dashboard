How to paste into Slack Canvas

  

    
1
Click Generate Canvas from Live Data above to fetch fresh numbers from Salesforce.

    
2
Click Copy to Clipboard.

    
3
Open your Slack canvas. Click inside the canvas body, press Ctrl+A to select all existing content, then Delete to clear it.

    
4
Press Ctrl+V to paste. Slack renders headings, bullets and tables automatically.
