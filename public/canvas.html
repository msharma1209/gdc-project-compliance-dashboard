How to paste into Slack Canvas

  

    
1
Click Generate Canvas from Live Data above.

    
2
Click Copy to Clipboard.

    
3
Open your Slack canvas → click inside it → press Ctrl+A to select all existing content → Delete to clear.

    
4
Press Ctrl+V to paste. Slack renders headings and bullets automatically.
